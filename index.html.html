Fluffy Friends

Drop the cuddly critters. When two of the same touch, they hug and grow into a bigger friend!

How to play

  • Slide to aim, then tap or click to drop. Keyboard: arrows move, space drops.
  • Two of the same friend that touch merge into the next one.
  • Chain reactions earn combo points.
  • Reach the crowned Giant Fluffy Bear. Two of them make a big bonus.
  • Keep every friend below the dotted line, or the jar overflows.
  • Wait too long and everyone falls asleep. Drop one to wake them.
